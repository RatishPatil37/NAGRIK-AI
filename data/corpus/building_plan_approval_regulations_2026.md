# Municipal Building Plan Approval & Setback Regulations 2026
**Document ID**: `MNC-TNP-2026-022`  
**Department**: Town Planning & Urban Architecture (`TNP`)  
**Effective Date**: 1st March 2026  
**Reference**: Unified Development Control Regulations (UDCR)

---

## 1. Single-Window Online Clearance (OBPAS)
1. All residential building plan approvals for plots up to 500 sq. meters are processed via the automated *Online Building Permission Approval System (OBPAS)* within a statutory SLA of **15 working days**.

## 2. Setback & Margin Norms
1. **Front Margin**: Minimum 3.0 meters from street edge for residential plots with road width < 12 meters; 4.5 meters for road width >= 12 meters.
2. **Rear & Side Margins**: Minimum 2.25 meters or H/5 (whichever is greater) for adequate ventilation and natural lighting.

## 3. Unauthorized Construction & Encroachment
1. Any construction exceeding approved Floor Space Index (FSI) or encroaching on public road margins shall be issued a statutory show-cause notice under Section 260 of the Municipal Corporation Act, returnable in **7 days**.
