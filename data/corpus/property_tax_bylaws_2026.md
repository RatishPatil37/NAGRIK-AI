# Municipal Corporation Property Tax Assessment & Rebate Bylaws 2026
**Document ID**: `MNC-REV-2026-001`  
**Department**: Revenue & Property Taxation (`REV`)  
**Effective Date**: 1st April 2026  
**Gazette Notification**: No. 104/UDD/2026

---

## Section 1: Short Title and Commencement
1. These bylaws shall be called the *Municipal Corporation Capital Value Property Tax Assessment Bylaws, 2026*.
2. They shall apply to all residential, commercial, industrial, and vacant land parcels within the municipal corporation territorial limits.

## Section 4: Capital Value Determination & Tax Rates
1. Property tax shall be computed as a percentage of the Ready Reckoner / Capital Value of the property:
   - **Residential Properties (Zone A & B)**: Base general tax rate of **1.2% per annum** of the assessed capital value.
   - **Commercial Properties (Zone A & B)**: Base general tax rate of **1.8% per annum**.
   - **Industrial & Storage Units**: Base rate of **1.5% per annum**.
   - **Vacant Land**: Assessed at **0.8% per annum** of base circle rate.

## Section 8: Rebates, Concessions and Incentives
1. **Early Bird Online Payment Rebate**: A seasonal rebate of **10% on the general tax component** is granted to property owners who discharge their complete annual property tax liability before **May 31st** through the municipal citizen portal or official mobile app.
2. **Green Building Rebate**: Certified Platinum/Gold green residential complexes with functioning rainwater harvesting and rooftop solar photovoltaic systems are eligible for an additional **5% annual tax rebate**.
3. **Senior Citizen Concession**: Residential self-occupied units owned by citizens aged 65 years and above receive a **5% concession** on presentation of valid age verification.

## Section 14: Appeals and Dispute Redressal
1. Any citizen disputing the assessed carpet area or property categorization may lodge an appeal before the Assistant Municipal Commissioner (Revenue) within **30 days** of receiving the assessment notice.
