# Municipal Water Supply Tariff, Metering & Leak Redressal Charter 2026
**Document ID**: `MNC-WTR-2026-014`  
**Department**: Water Supply & Sewerage Department (`WTR`)  
**Effective Date**: 1st January 2026  
**Authority**: Chief Hydraulic Engineer

---

## 1. Domestic Water Supply Standards
1. The Municipal Corporation commits to supplying potable drinking water conforming to **BIS 10500 standards** at an average pressure of not less than **0.8 bar** at consumer terminal points.
2. Minimum daily domestic per capita supply entitlement is benchmarked at **135 Liters Per Capita per Day (LPCD)**.

## 2. Tariff & Slab Rates (Domestic Metered)
- **Slab 1 (0 to 15,000 Liters / Month)**: Subsidized civic rate of **INR 6.00 per 1,000 Liters**.
- **Slab 2 (15,001 to 30,000 Liters / Month)**: Standard rate of **INR 12.00 per 1,000 Liters**.
- **Slab 3 (Above 30,000 Liters / Month)**: Conservation penalty rate of **INR 25.00 per 1,000 Liters**.

## 3. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for Redressal
1. **Emergency Burst Pipeline / Severe Contamination**:
   - Redressal SLA: **4 Hours**.
   - Immediate dispatch of mobile repair gang and suspension of contaminated feeder valve.
   - Alternative mobile drinking water tanker dispatched to affected street within 90 minutes.
2. **Low Pressure / Irregular Timing Complaints**:
   - Inspection SLA: **24 Hours**.
3. **Water Meter Fault / Billing Disputes**:
   - Resolution SLA: **7 Days**.
