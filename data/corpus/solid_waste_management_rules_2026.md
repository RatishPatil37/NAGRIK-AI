# Solid Waste Management & Sanitation Bylaws 2026
**Document ID**: `MNC-SAN-2026-009`  
**Department**: Solid Waste Management & Public Health (`SAN`)  
**Effective Date**: 1st February 2026  
**Framework**: National SWM Rules (Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change)

---

## Section 3: Segregation at Source
1. Every waste generator (household, gated society, commercial establishment) shall segregate waste at source into three distinct streams:
   - **Wet Waste (Green Bin)**: Biodegradable organic matter, raw/cooked food waste.
   - **Dry Waste (Blue Bin)**: Recyclable paper, plastic, glass, metal, packaging.
   - **Domestic Hazardous Waste (Red Bin)**: Sanitary napkins, expired medicines, batteries, fluorescent bulbs.

## Section 12: Penalties for Non-Compliance
1. **Littering / Open Dumping**: On-spot municipal challan of **INR 500** for first offense, increasing to **INR 1,000** for repeat violations.
2. **Failure to Segregate**: Residential societies failing source segregation after 2 written warnings are subject to a sanitation penalty of **INR 5,000 per month**.
3. **Burning of Solid Waste / Leaves**: Open burning in public spaces carries an immediate statutory environmental compensation fee of **INR 5,000**.
